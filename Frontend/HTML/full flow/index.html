Popular Destinations

Chennai — The Gateway to South India

About:

Chennai is a vibrant coastal city known for its rich Tamil culture, beautiful beaches, traditional temples, music, art, and delicious South Indian cuisine.

Top Attractions:

1. Marina Beach
2.Kapaleeshwarar Temple
3. Fort St. George
4.Government Museum
5. San Thome Basilica

Best For:

Culture, beaches, food, history, and family trips.

Ooty — Queen of Hill Stations

About:

Ooty is a peaceful hill station surrounded by green mountains, tea plantations, forests, and beautiful landscapes.

Top Attractions:

1. Ooty Lake
2.Botanical Gardens
3.Doddabetta Peak
4. Rose Garden
5. Coonoor Tea Plantations

Best For:

Nature, trekking, photography, and romantic getaways.

Goa — The Beach Paradise

About:

Goa is a tropical coastal state in India known for its beautiful beaches, vibrant nightlife, and rich Portuguese heritage.

Top Attractions:

1.Calangute Beach
2.Dona Paula
3.Shiwalik Hills
4.Basilica of Bom Jesus
5.Mapusa Market

Best For:

Beaches, nightlife, culture, and family trips.

Jaipur — The Pink City

About:

Jaipur is the capital city of Rajasthan, known for its stunning architecture, rich history, and vibrant culture.

Top Attractions:

1.Amber Fort
2.City Palace
3.Hawa Mahal
4.Jantar Mantar
5.Birla Museum

Best For:

History, culture, architecture, and family trips.
